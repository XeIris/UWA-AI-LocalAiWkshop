The through-line of this entire workshop

The same 15 GB model, three machines Estimated tokens / sec
DDR5 system RAM 80 GB/s ~5 tok/s
M‑series unified memory 400 GB/s ~27 tok/s
Discrete GPU VRAM 900 GB/s ~60 tok/s

Order‑of‑magnitude teaching aids, not benchmarks — and all three are optimistic. Real output lands meaningfully lower.
