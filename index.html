00 — Why bother?

Five reasons that survive scrutiny

Privacy

It never leaves the room

Your notes, your clients’ data, your medical questions. No request is made, so there is nothing to log, leak or subpoena.

Offline

Works on a plane

No network, no outage, no “service is at capacity.” The model is a file on your disk.

No limits

No rate limits, no meter

Run it all night on ten thousand documents. The only cost is electricity and your patience.

Control

Swap the brain out

Pick a different model for a different job. Fine‑tune one. Keep a version that will never be deprecated out from under you.

Unfiltered

No content filtering

Useful for fiction, security research and medicine, where hosted models often refuse legitimate work.
