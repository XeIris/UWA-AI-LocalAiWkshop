Estimates, not benchmarks. Every row is the same 8B model at 4‑bit — 4.9 GB — divided into the bandwidth beside it. Measured on llama.cpp the two fast machines hit 186 and 85 tok/s — about half of the ceiling, and about two thirds. Bandwidth sets the ceiling, not the number, and that is still the only thing on the page that predicts the answer.
