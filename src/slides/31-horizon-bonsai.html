Bonsai (PrismML, Apache 2.0) is trained with {−1, 0, +1} weights from the start — never a 16‑bit model that got rounded, so there is nothing to lose on the way down: 8 B in 1.75 GB, benchmark average 75.5 against 70.5 for the earlier 1‑bit version that had no zero. The catch is a version number — PrismML ship two ternary builds, and only the group‑64 one (*-Q2_0_g64.gguf) is the format mainline llama.cpp took: CPU, Metal and Vulkan are merged, CUDA is still in review, and the older group‑128 files need their fork. Whether the llama.cpp inside your LM Studio is new enough is a question to settle before tonight, not during it. The decode figure is §3’s formula on a published bandwidth, so it is a ceiling: for ternary PrismML measure 82 tok/s on an M4 Pro and 27 on an iPhone 17 Pro Max, roughly half to four fifths of it.
