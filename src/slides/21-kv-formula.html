GQA is why your arithmetic will over-estimate. The naive formula gives every attention head its own key and value; modern models share one KV head between groups of them, cutting the cache several-fold. Toggle it and watch the line pivot. Some models cut further still with sliding-window layers, which cap the cache instead of growing it.
