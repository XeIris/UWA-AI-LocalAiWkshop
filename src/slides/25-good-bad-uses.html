A small model is a tool, not a smaller genius

The pattern is one line: bring the knowledge to the model. Everything on the left hands it the material; everything on the right asks it to supply the material itself. That is why retrieval over your own files works far better than a 1 B model has any right to, and why “what do you know about …” disappoints. The right column is a difference of degree, not of kind — frontier models still hallucinate on 3–19% of fact-seeking questions depending on the model and the task, and error still compounds across a long run for everyone. A small model just reaches the edge sooner, and says nothing when it gets there.
