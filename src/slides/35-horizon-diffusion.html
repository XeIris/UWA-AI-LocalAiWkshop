Where the bottleneck went Autoregressive decode reads every weight once per token. Diffusion reads them once per denoising pass, and one pass moves all 256 tokens forward — sixteen passes, not 256 — so the memory bus stops being the wall, and arithmetic becomes it instead.

This is the formula from §3 breaking. tok/s ≈ bandwidth ÷ model size held all the way through because one pass produced one token. Denoise a block instead and one pass advances 256 of them, so the same read of the weights buys sixteen tokens rather than one — which is why a Mac’s bandwidth advantage would matter less here, and raw compute more. The catch is the hardware. DiffusionGemma (google/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it) is a 26 B mixture of experts with 3.8 B active, and Google put the quantized weights inside 24 GB of VRAM: a 4090‑class graphics card. That is a desk, not a lap, and well past anything downloaded here today. Apache 2.0, weights on Hugging Face, and it is served on Vertex AI if you want to try it without the card. Rates are Google’s figures on a single H100, not on anything in this room.
