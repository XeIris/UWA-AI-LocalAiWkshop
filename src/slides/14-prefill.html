The asymmetry The Mac is 2.2× behind on writing — and 11.6× behind on reading.

Prefill is arithmetic; decode is memory traffic. The 5090 has roughly three times the Mac's bandwidth but more than ten times its useful matrix compute — so which phase you are in decides how big the gap feels. Figures are for an 8 B model at 4‑bit; the Mac's prefill is the conservative end of a wide range, because MLX uses the M5's neural accelerators and llama.cpp does not yet. Attention is also quadratic, so a really long prompt is worse than this straight line suggests.
