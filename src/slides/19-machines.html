Both machines hold 64 GB, and that is the point: capacity is not the number that decides speed. Drag past 16B and watch the PC fall off a cliff the Mac does not have. Then drop to 4‑bit and watch the cliff move. Weights only. The bars count nothing but the model. So the opening state, 32 GB of weights in 32 GB of VRAM, is the theoretical edge rather than a configuration to copy: the KV cache of §5 and a couple of gigabytes of runtime have to live there too, and in practice that machine falls off this cliff a little before the slider says it does.
