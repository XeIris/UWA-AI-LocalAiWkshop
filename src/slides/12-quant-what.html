Note the shared scale. A quant does not store 24 tiny numbers — it stores 24 tiny integers plus one full-precision multiplier for the block. That multiplier is why a “4‑bit” file is never exactly 4 bits per weight, and real GGUF blocks carry more metadata than this one. Hold that thought for one slide.
