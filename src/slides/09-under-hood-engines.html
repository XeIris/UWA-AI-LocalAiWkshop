02 — Under the hood

LM Studio is a wrapper. These do the work.

llama.cpp

The engine almost everything runs on. C++, no dependencies, runs on anything from a Raspberry Pi to a server. Reads GGUF.

EVERY PLATFORM

MLX

Apple’s own array framework, built for unified memory. Meaningfully faster than llama.cpp on a Mac, useless anywhere else.

APPLE SILICON ONLY

Worth knowing because it means the app is not the magic — it is a face on top of an engine, and you can change either one.
