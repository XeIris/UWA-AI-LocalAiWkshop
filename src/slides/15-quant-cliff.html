The sweet spot. Where almost everyone should live.

Small models fall further. A 30B has parameters to spare and shrugs off rounding; a 1B does not, which matters tonight because a 1B is what most of you downloaded. Below 4‑bit the failure is invisible — no error, no warning, just quiet loops, contradictions and confident nonsense. Shape of the curve: real. Exact percentages: illustrative, and they move with every model and benchmark.
