Where to go next

This week
  1. Run the same prompt through both. The 1 B you downloaded and a 4 B. The gap is the whole of §6 in ninety seconds.
  2. Point it at your own PDF. Attach a document and ask about it. That is the thing a small model is genuinely good at.
  3. Measure your own machine. LM Studio reports tok/s. Check it against bandwidth ÷ file size and see how much of the ceiling you actually get.
Rabbit holes we skipped
  • Speculative decoding — a small model drafts, a big one checks. Real speedups, heavy concepts.
  • Embeddings and a proper RAG stack — what tonight’s “chat with your files” is doing underneath.
  • Fine-tuning and LoRA — changing the weights rather than the prompt. Start here only after RAG has failed you.
  • Serving it — LM Studio and Ollama both expose a local API, which is how you point other software at your own model.

One honest closing note. Half the specifics in this deck will be wrong within a year — the model names certainly, the hardware probably. The arithmetic will not be: a model is a file, it has to fit, and it is read once per token. That is what you came for.
